A sunlit coastal crime adventure in the original city of Solara Bay. Walk the boardwalk, commandeer cars, outrun patrols, take on three story jobs, or cruise the shoreline. All visuals and music are generated locally by the game.

## Run it

Run `node serve.ts` from the repository, then open http://localhost:8080. Use WASD or arrows to move and drive, E to enter/exit cars, mouse to aim, click or Space to fire, and M to start the boardwalk sprint. Progress saves in your browser. Append `?shot=start`, `?shot=drive`, or `?shot=mission` for staged scenes.
